## 3. Visual Consistency & Institutional Design Audit

The website was audited to verify that the **Quiet Authority** design system is cohesively applied without falling into visual monotony:

### A. Palette Architecture
- **Warm Porcelain (`#F5F5F0`):** Used consistently across primary content containers, cards, and technical badges for crisp readability.
- **Graphite (`#171A18`):** Deep, editorial dark surfaces replace cheap `#000000` pitch blacks and cyan-purple tinted backdrops.
- **Signal Lime (`#B8F36B`):** Reserved exclusively for verified states, active indicators, and high-value technical signals.
- **Muted Sage (`#647A6A`):** Grounds secondary metadata, colophons, and subtle borders.
- **Ambient Radiant Lighting:** `AmbientGlow.tsx` has been purged of cyan/purple neon and calibrated to subtle Signal Lime / Warm Porcelain atmospheric radiance.

### B. Typographical Discipline
- Interface elements, eyebrows, table headers, breadcrumbs, and CTAs strictly use **Geist / Inter**.
- Monospace typography is strictly quarantined to genuine technical content:
  - CLI commands (`git worktree add`, `eleven-cli`)
  - File paths (`.eleven/policy.yaml`, `quarantine_worker.rs`)
  - Telemetry counters (`<50ms`, `60 FPS`, `SHA-256`)
  - Cryptographic hashes (`ed25519_pk`, `entry_hash = SHA256(...)`)
- All glued arrows (`→` concatenated to label text) were eliminated across buttons and navigation links.
